## Style & World Guide

### Time Period: High-Fantasy Pseudo-Medieval
Voxelforge takes place in an original high-fantasy world inspired by the spirit and eras of *Warcraft*'s Azeroth (using our own original lore, names, and creatures—no Blizzard IP):
- **The Core Era:** The structural foundation is rooted in the Late Middle Ages and Renaissance (approx. 14th to 16th century), featuring castles, knights, plate armor, and feudal kingdoms.
- **Technological Anachronisms:** The timeline deviates wildly from strict medieval boundaries due to engineering and magic. You will routinely encounter early industrial steam power, black powder firearms, flying machines, and arcane/mechanical constructs (like the M.G.R.S.).

### Visual & Artistic Style: Stylized Hand-Painted & Comic Fantasy
- **"Chunky" Proportions:** Characters, weapons, armor, and voxel props feature exaggerated, oversized geometry. Shoulders are massive, blades are impossibly thick, and silhouettes are bold and easily identifiable from a distance against the 1m voxel grid.
- **Hand-Painted Textures:** Both voxel block faces and 3D prop/character meshes rely on illustrative, hand-drawn/procedural painterly texture maps rather than photorealistic noise, giving the world a colorful, canvas-like feel.
- **High Contrast, Saturated Color & Comic Framing:** The palette is bright and saturated, leaning into a bold comic-book / dark fairy-tale aesthetic (backed by inked frames, halftone accents, and expressive typography in the HUD) that separates it from grittier, monochromatic fantasy games.

## Phase 6: The Tavern Door (Front-End, Loading Screen & Settings)
**Goal:** Build a polished front-end flow, an asynchronous chunk-streaming loading screen, and scalable video/display settings before opening the game to online PCs.
- [ ] **6.1 Title Screen, World Picker & Character Creation Flow:** Build the front-end state machine transitioning from Title Screen → World Selection (create new world with `WorldSeed` or load existing save from 3.4) → Per-Player Couch Co-Op Lobby & Character Creation (each local player joins on their own `InputUser` device from 2.2 and selects or creates a profile with Race from 4.7 and Class from 4.6). Each profile gets a player-chosen hero name, shown over heroes and in the HUD, and the 4.5 inventory's **Send to Partner** gains a target picker by name when 3-4 players are in the game (until then it sends to the nearest partner).
- [ ] **6.2 Async Loading Screen & Chunk Readiness Gate:** Implement a loading screen between front-end launch and active gameplay that reports world initialization, initial chunk generation and greedy meshing progress around all viewer positions (2.4), and network delta sync progress (7.2), holding player physics until starting terrain colliders are ready.
- [ ] **6.3 Screen, Display & Audio Settings:** Build persistent machine-level display and audio settings covering Resolution, Window Mode (Exclusive Fullscreen, Borderless Window, Windowed), Monitor selection, VSync, Frame Rate Cap, UI Scale, Split-Screen camera preferences (such as the Voronoi split distance threshold from 2.3), and Master / SFX / Ambience / Music volume sliders (plus Mute on Focus Loss) wired into the 3.9 audio router.
- [ ] **6.4 Video Quality & Voxel Performance Settings:** Add graphics quality presets (Low / Medium / High / Ultra) and granular sliders/toggles wired into URP and the voxel engine: Chunk Streaming Distance (2.4), Foliage Density & Draw Distance (2.6 `RenderMeshIndirect`), URP Render Scale, Shadow Distance & Cascades, Anti-Aliasing, Parallax Mapping (1.5), and Voxel Corner AO (2.6).
- [ ] **6.5 In-Game Pause & Settings Menu:** Add a per-player-aware Pause/Options overlay accessible in-game (via the initiating `InputUser` without single-player shortcuts) to adjust Screen, Audio & Video Quality settings live, trigger a manual save (3.4), or cleanly shut down the local Listen-Server and return to the Title Screen.
- [ ] **6.6 Title Diorama, Loading Art & Menu Framing (Creative):** Create a live 3D voxel diorama backdrop for the Title and Character Creation screens (a stylized camp/tavern scene with swaying foliage, torchlight, and glowing ore), stylized RPG menu frames, loading bar art with gameplay tips, and menu/loading ambient audio.
- [ ] **6.7 Playable Demo Build:** Build a new Windows `.exe` (version `0.6.x`, same `DemoBuilder` as 2.7) and play-test the full front-end-to-gameplay loop outside the editor: booting to the Title Screen, changing window mode/resolution, audio volume sliders, and video quality presets, creating a world and two heroes, transitioning through the loading screen into split-screen gameplay, tweaking chunk/foliage/audio settings live from the Pause Menu, and returning to Title to reload the saved world. Verify 120+ FPS at default quality settings and record results in `DEVLOG.md`.

## Phase 7: Opening the Portal (Internet Multiplayer)
**Goal:** Enable online co-op with friends while preserving local Dynamic Split-Screen.
- [ ] **7.1 Relay Transport Swap:** Integrate Steamworks P2P or Epic Online Services (EOS) transport layer (no port-forwarding required) and wire Host/Join lobby flows into the 6.1 Title Screen.
- [ ] **7.2 Initial World Sync:** Send `WorldSeed` + compressed `VoxelDeltaLog` to joining remote players upon connection, feeding sync progress into the 6.2 Loading Screen before spawning.
- [ ] **7.3 Bandwidth Optimization:** Compress chunk delta broadcasts and test mixed mode (2 local split-screen players + 1-2 remote online players).
